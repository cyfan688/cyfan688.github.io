About Me

I am a third-year undergraduate student at Stony Brook University after completing two years of study at Anhui University. My primary research interest is offline reinforcement learning.

My interest in AI began with large language models and their potential to make educational support more accessible. Growing up in Huangshan, where educational resources were relatively limited, made this possibility especially meaningful to me. I later became interested in reinforcement learning after seeing agents learn through trial and error in games and robotics.

I am particularly interested in offline reinforcement learning, which learns from previously collected data when online interaction is costly, risky, or impractical. I am currently building a solid foundation by reproducing classic algorithms and reading relevant textbooks and research papers. This website records my learning process and will be updated as I work on new projects.
